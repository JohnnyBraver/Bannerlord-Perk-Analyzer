# Personal Non-Combat Perks

This note covers perks with `personal` role that are not primarily about the player's live-battle damage, defense, accuracy, or weapon handling.

Combat-relevant unique unlocks such as horseback bows, horseback crossbow reload, and shield-piercing javelins are discussed in `personal-combat.md`. Smithing mechanics are summarized here only briefly; the detailed XP, value, refining, and part-research notes live in `smithing.md`. Detailed shop-price, trade-penalty, and economy routing notes live in `trade-economy.md`.

## Main Takeaways

- The best personal non-combat perks are often quality-of-life or build-shaping perks, not raw power perks.
- Trade 25 remains one of the best one-focus splashes in the game because price/profit marking changes the player's information layer immediately.
- Permanent attribute perks from Athletics and Smithing can reshape whole builds because attribute points help three skills at once.
- Some very powerful unlocks are extremely expensive. `Everything Has a Price` is unique, but Trade 300 is a high-investment endpoint, not a casual pickup.

## Character Growth

These are the most build-warping personal non-combat perks because they add permanent points.

| Skill | Level | Perk | Effect | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Smithing | 150 | `Vigorous Smith` | +1 Vigor | Helps One Handed, Two Handed, and Polearm. |
| Smithing | 150 | `Controlled Smith` | +1 Control | Helps Bow, Crossbow, and Throwing. |
| Athletics | 175 | `Durable` | +1 Endurance | Helps Athletics, Riding, and Smithing. |
| Athletics | 200 | `Strong` | +1 Vigor | Strong if the build also wants melee. |
| Athletics | 200 | `Steady` | +1 Control | Strong if the build also wants ranged. |
| Smithing | 225 | `Enduring Smith` | +1 Endurance | Usually part of an Endurance-heavy stretch plan. |
| Smithing | 225 | `Fencer Smith` | +1 focus to One Handed and Two Handed | Narrower than an attribute point, but useful for Vigor melee builds. |

The key is shared value. A single +1 Vigor is much better if the build also uses two or three Vigor skills. This is why these perks should be evaluated at the build level, not as isolated skill rewards.

## Trade Information And Economy

### Trade Information

The early Trade information perks are deceptively valuable because they reduce player friction every time the trade screen opens.

| Skill | Level | Perk | Effect | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Trade | 25 | `Appraiser` / `Whole Seller` | Profits are marked. | Excellent one-focus quality-of-life pickup. |
| Trade | 50 | `Caravan Master` / `Market Dealer` | Prices are marked relative to average price. | Helps decide what to buy, not only what to sell. |
| Trade | 100 | `Traveling Rumors` | Caravans gather trade rumors. | Useful if using caravans. |
| Trade | 100 | `Toll Gates` | Workshops gather trade rumors. | Useful if using workshops. |

The level 25 Trade splash is especially strong because `2 attribute + 1 focus` reaches the 25 and 50 perk tiers. It is not about making the character richer by formula alone; it makes the player make better trade decisions.

| Riding | 50 | `Well Strapped` | -50% chance your mount dies or becomes lame after falling. | Good if the player relies on expensive mounts. |

`Everything Has a Price` deserves special caution. It is probably the most famous personal non-combat unlock, but Trade 300 costs far more than a normal splash. It makes sense for a Trade identity build, not for a normal combat lord who merely wants better shop prices.

Other relationship sources:

- `Medicine Best Medicine` and `Good Lodging`: daily relationship with older notables/nobles while in town.
- `Smithing Experienced Smith` and `Steel Maker 3`: relationship from successful crafting orders.
- `Trade Distributed Goods` and `Local Connection`: doubled relationship gain from artisan/merchant issues.
- `One Handed Duelist`: doubles tournament renown.
- `Leadership Famous Commander`: +50% renown from battles.

Roguery personal perks support a criminal or infiltration style:

| Skill | Level | Perk | Effect |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Roguery | 50 | `Two Faced` | +50% chance for sneaking into towns. |
| Roguery | 125 | `Scarface` | Chance for bandits, villagers, and caravans to surrender. |

These are powerful only if the campaign is actually using criminal mechanics. Otherwise they are easy to overvalue.

## Crafting And Smithing

Smithing has many personal non-combat perks, but it is its own subsystem. The short version:

- `Efficient Charcoal Maker` is excellent early Smithing skill XP because refining XP uses output value and output count.
- `Curious Smelter` and `Curious Smith` improve part-learning, not Smithing skill XP directly.
- `Practical Refiner`, `Practical Smelter`, and `Practical Smith` reduce stamina cost for their respective actions.
- `Experienced Smith`, `Master Smith`, and `Legendary Smith` improve quality chances.
- Steel-maker perks unlock higher material refinement chains.

See `smithing.md` for the value formulas, XP formulas, and crafting optimization notes.

## Practical Reads

Best low-investment personal non-combat splashes:

- Trade 25 for profit marking.
- Medicine 25 for the post-battle personal healing package if it fits the build.
- Athletics 50 `Form Fitting Armor` if armor weight matters.
- Charm 25/50 if the campaign is tournament- or issue-heavy.
- Roguery 50 if tournaments, bandit wages, or sneaking matter.

Best build-defining personal non-combat investments:

- Smithing 150/225 and Athletics 175/200 attribute perks.
- Trade 300 if settlement barter is the campaign goal.
- Charm 200+ if persuasion and clan recruitment are central.
- Roguery 150+ if disguise/crime play is central.

Things to avoid overvaluing:

- Late perks that only solve rare failure cases, unless those failures are ruining the campaign.
- Relationship perks for NPC groups the player does not actively interact with.
- Trade penalties for goods the player does not actually trade.
- Smithing quality perks if the player is not already deep into Smithing.
